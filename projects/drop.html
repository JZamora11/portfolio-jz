Retail graphic design and high-impact visual composition for the launch of the Air Jordan 4 collection.

01. Project Challenge

The main objective of this project was to create a high-impact retail poster for a Jordan brand product release. The visual challenge was to integrate bold typography and UI elements over a complex texture without compromising readability or the premium presentation of the product.

02. Strategy & Technical Development

Using a product-first layout over a grey wood-grain texture, I structured the information for immediate impact. Solutions included applying outlined typography and precise kerning to the main headline, creating contrast against the dark background while highlighting the product itself.

03. Final Polish & Optimization

To finalize the asset, I focused on visual hierarchy. Integrated a realistic UI search bar and placed the product and brand logos at the bottom for professional branding. The final poster delivers immediate brand recognition and clear call-to-actions within a sophisticated, textured environment, ready for digital distribution.
